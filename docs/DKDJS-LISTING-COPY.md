## 6. Things to have ready

- **Logo** — square, at least 400×400
- **Cover image** — 1200×630, a real event photo, not a stock nightclub DJ
- **Photo set** — 5 to 10 images; most directories rank profiles with more photos higher
- **Business hours** — most directories require them; "by appointment" is a valid answer for a mobile service
- **Year established** — leave blank rather than guessing. A wrong date is worse than none.

---

## 7. What NOT to claim

Do not tick boxes or fill fields for anything not yet true:

- Liability insurance / certificate of insurance — not in place yet
- Years in business or number of events — no figure has been settled
- Awards, certifications, memberships — none yet
- Languages beyond English, unless accurate

A directory profile claiming insurance you do not carry is a real problem, not a marketing flourish.
