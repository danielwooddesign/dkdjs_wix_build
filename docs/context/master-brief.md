## Operative summary (what the code enforces today)

**Positioning.** "Fun, professional music & entertainment without the
nightclub-DJ attitude." Promise: "Great Music. Unforgettable Events."
Differentiator: "You Get the Two of Us. Every Time."

**Service area.** Boise, Eagle, Meridian, Nampa, Kuna, Star, Caldwell,
Garden City and surrounding Treasure Valley communities.

**Navigation (§4).** Home · Weddings · Events · Karaoke · Packages · Gallery ·
About · **Check Your Date** (highlighted CTA). Footer: Our Setup, Equipment
Rentals, FAQ, Contact, Service Areas, Privacy, Terms.
*Open: line dancing has a homepage card and a page but no nav slot.*

**Homepage order (§6).** Hero → service-area/trust bar → More Than Music →
What We Do → What Comes With Us → Straight Pricing → LED Video Experience →
Reviews → Gallery → final Check Your Date CTA → footer.

**Hero (§7).** Eyebrow "TREASURE VALLEY DJ & MC". H1 "NOBODY REMEMBERS THE
CENTERPIECES. THEY REMEMBER THE DANCE FLOOR." Primary CTA "CHECK YOUR DATE",
secondary "VIEW PACKAGES". Optional date checker must not overwhelm the
emotional message.

**Wedding pricing (§14).** The Reception $1,095 / 4 hrs · **The Full Day
$1,595 / 6 hrs (MOST BOOKED)** · The Whole Night $2,095 / 8 hrs.

**Non-wedding (§15).** Private party $595 / 3 hrs · Karaoke night $450 /
3 hrs · Corporate or holiday $895 / 4 hrs · Ceremony audio only $395 ·
Restaurant residency custom recurring.

**LED booth pricing (§16).** Booth +$349 · Custom animated visuals +$250 ·
Booth + custom experience +$599 · Corporate branded from $595 · Restaurant
content updates from $150/month.

**Add-ons (§17).** Extra hour $175 · Karaoke hour $150 · Enhanced uplighting
$295 · Custom monogram $195. Cold sparks / dance-floor effects **not
advertised** until equipment, venue restrictions, insurance and operations
are confirmed.

**Equipment (§12, amended 2026-09-14).** JBL PartyBox Ultimate ×2 (1100W
each) · PartyBox Stage 320 ×2 · PartyBox Encore ×2 (~100W each) ·
PartyLight Stick ×4 · PartyLight Beam ×2 · professional speaker stands ·
JBL wireless microphones · curved LED video DJ booth. Setup scales to venue
size. JBL stated factually only — no implied sponsorship or endorsement.

**LED booth (§13).** Position as "Custom LED Visual Experience", never "a DJ
facade". Displays names, monograms, photos, logos, event colors, promotions,
QR codes, sponsor and venue content.

**Payments (§30).** Processed through **New Ad City**, Daniel's umbrella
company. Site stays branded DKDJS. Disclose near the payment step so the
statement descriptor isn't a surprise. Use Wix's payment system, not a custom
processor.

**Deposit / retainer (§31).** Deliberately undefined. Terminology and refund
policy must stay configurable. **Do not hard-code refund terms.**

**Development priority (§40).** Brand/design system → global header/footer →
homepage → packages → weddings → events → karaoke → about → gallery → check
date → event builder → quote path → payment/deposit → email confirmations →
FAQ → our setup → rentals → SEO/schema → location pages → client portal →
guest QR requests.

**Business priority (§41).** Brand → credibility → content → conversion →
SEO → automation. The site sells Daniel & Kathy first.
