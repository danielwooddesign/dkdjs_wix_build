# DKDJS — AI Visibility Questions

For Wix **Analytics → AI Visibility Overview → Manage Questions**.

Wix runs each question against ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, then reports the percentage where dkdjs.com got mentioned or cited. Limits: **50 questions stored**, **one test run per day**, and the dashboard needs **50 unique visitors in the last 30 days** before it shows data.

There are 43 questions below, leaving 7 slots free. Delete the ones Wix auto-generated first — they're built from your site description and tend to be vague.

---

## The thing to understand before you paste these in

**The score is a function of the questions you choose.** Load it with "Who are Daniel & Kathy DJs?" and you'll score 90% and learn nothing. Load it with "best wedding DJ in Boise" and you'll score near zero for months. Neither number tells you anything useful.

The set below is deliberately mixed so the score is a *diagnostic* rather than a vanity metric. Roughly:

- **4 brand questions** — your control group. If these ever fail, something is broken.
- **20 commercial questions** — the ones you want to win. Expect to lose most of these at first.
- **19 informational questions** — the ones you can realistically win *this year*.

That last group is the important one, and it points at a gap. When someone asks ChatGPT "who is the best wedding DJ in Boise," the answer gets assembled from The Knot, WeddingWire, Yelp, Google Business Profile and Reddit threads — **not** from DJ company websites. You essentially cannot win those questions with site content alone.

What you *can* win is "how far in advance should I book a wedding DJ in Idaho" — a question with a real answer, where a well-written page gets quoted directly. Right now DKDJS has no page that answers any of the 19 informational questions below. That's the finding this exercise is going to hand you, and it's worth acting on: a genuine FAQ or wedding-planning page is the cheapest path to getting cited.

## How to run this so it's actually useful

**Freeze a core ten and never change them.** I'd use: the four brand questions, "Who is the best wedding DJ in Boise, Idaho?", "How much does a wedding DJ cost in Boise, Idaho?", "Are there any Boise wedding DJs who list their prices online?", "Are there any husband-and-wife DJ teams in Boise, Idaho?", "How far in advance should I book a wedding DJ in Idaho?", and "Where can I hire a karaoke DJ in Boise?" Those ten are your trend line. Rotating your whole set every month makes the score meaningless because you're measuring a different thing each time.

**Write the date and score down.** Wix shows you today's number; it's not great at showing you the trajectory. A four-line note each month is enough.

**Expect a low first number.** The site launched days ago. A score in single digits is the correct result, not a problem to fix. What matters is whether it moves.

**The lever is still off-site.** Google Business Profile, then The Knot and WeddingWire listings, then reviews. Those are the sources the models actually read for "best DJ in Boise" questions. No amount of site editing substitutes for them.

**Don't chase the score by rewording questions.** The temptation, three months in, will be to swap the hard questions for easy ones. That converts a diagnostic into a dashboard ornament.
