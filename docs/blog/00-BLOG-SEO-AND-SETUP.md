## Why these three

They are not arbitrary. Each one answers a question from your AI Visibility list that the site currently has no page for, and each targets a different stage of the buying decision.

**Post 1 is the money page.** "How much does a wedding DJ cost in Boise" is high-volume, high-intent, and plays to the one structural advantage you have: you publish every number. It anchors against the published Idaho 2026 figure — average wedding $26,000, entertainment 4%, about $1,100 — which happens to sit almost exactly on The Reception at $1,095. That is a genuinely strong position and the article says so without overselling it.

**Post 2 creates urgency honestly.** One event per day is a real constraint, not a scarcity tactic, so the article can make the point plainly. It also earns links from wedding-planning roundups, which is the kind of thing that actually moves rankings.

**Post 3 is the one AI models will quote.** Listicles with clear question-and-answer structure get lifted into ChatGPT and Perplexity answers more readily than anything else on a small site. The twelve questions are written to be genuinely useful whoever the reader hires — then the last section answers them for DKDJS, which is fair and considerably more persuasive than rigging each question.

## Publishing notes

**Delete the Wix placeholder posts before publishing, not after.** They are indexed the moment the blog goes live, and "3 Tips for Starting a Blog" appearing under dkdjs.com is worse than having no blog. If any were already indexed, deleting them leaves 404s — fine, but check Search Console in a couple of weeks.

**Publish them a week or two apart**, not all on one day. Three posts appearing simultaneously and then silence reads as abandoned. Staggered, it reads as a blog.

**Each post needs a featured image.** 1200×630 works for both Wix's feed and social sharing. Suggestions, in your existing photo style:

- Post 1 — a reception during dinner, lights down, room full. Alt text: `Wedding reception in Boise, Idaho with DJ setup and dance floor lighting`
- Post 2 — a calendar or planning shot, or an outdoor Treasure Valley venue in daylight. Alt text: `Outdoor wedding venue in the Treasure Valley, Idaho`
- Post 3 — Daniel and Kathy at the booth, mid-event. Alt text: `Daniel and Kathy DJs at the microphone during a Boise wedding reception`

Do not use stock photos of a nightclub DJ. It undercuts everything the site says about who you are.

**Internal links are already in the drafts** — each post links to `/packages`, `/check-availability` and, where relevant, `/weddings`. Check they survive the paste into Wix; the editor sometimes strips relative links.

**Every fact is traceable.** Prices come from `src/public/pricing.js`, the retainer and balance terms from `POLICY`, the service area from `CITIES`, and the Idaho average from published 2026 wedding cost data. Nothing about years in business, number of weddings, insurance or awards appears anywhere — those are yours to add once you decide what you want to claim.

**One caveat on Post 1.** It quotes your current prices in the body. If you change a package price, that article goes stale and will be quoting a number your own packages page contradicts. Worth a calendar note.
